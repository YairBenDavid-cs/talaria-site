Talaria – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Talaria ("the app", "we") is a personal training app. This policy explains what data we collect when you connect your Garmin account, how we use it, who processes it, and the choices you have.

1. Data we receive from Garmin

When you connect your Garmin Connect account and approve the permissions you choose, Garmin sends us data from your account. Depending on the permissions you grant, this can include:

  • Activities: workout summaries, detailed workout data and original activity files (time, distance, pace, heart rate, power, cadence, running dynamics, GPS route, laps, strength sets).
  • Health: daily summaries (steps, resting heart rate, intensity, calories), sleep, heart rate variability, respiration, pulse oximetry, skin temperature, stress and Body Battery, body composition (weight, body fat) and fitness metrics such as VO2 max.
  • Women's health (only if you share it): menstrual cycle data.

We also store the access tokens needed to keep your Garmin connection working, encrypted. We never ask for or store your Garmin password.

If you use the AI coach, we also keep what you write to it: your conversations, the coach's replies, the training plan it builds with you, and the facts it remembers about you (see section 3).

2. How we use your data

We use Garmin data only to provide the app's features to you:

  • Analyze your workouts, fitness and recovery and show you the results.
  • Build and adjust a personalized training plan based on your data.
  • Send structured workouts you choose to your Garmin device and compare planned vs. completed sessions.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not use it for credit, insurance or employment decisions.

3. AI Transparency Statement

Talaria uses artificial intelligence to analyze your training and health data and to suggest personalized training plans and workouts.

  • What is processed: your messages to the coach; the Garmin data listed in section 1 that is relevant to what you ask; a short summary we prepare for each conversation (your profile and goals, the facts the coach remembers, and your current training week); and, for remembering, the messages of a conversation after it goes quiet. We send no more than the conversation needs, and never a bulk export of your data.
  • Purpose: only to generate analysis and training recommendations for you.
  • Provider: AI processing is performed by Anthropic (Anthropic, PBC, the maker of Claude), acting as our service provider. Anthropic keeps the data it receives from us for up to 30 days, and the results of the nightly memory update for up to 29 days, and then deletes them.
  • What the coach remembers: the coach keeps short notes about you, such as your goals, injuries, availability and preferences, so it doesn't ask again. Some notes come from what you say; others it proposes, and you confirm them. In the app, "What your coach knows" lists every note: you can correct, confirm, reject or delete any of them at any time. When you delete a note, the coach stops using it at once. Its text may stay in our internal record of earlier conversation sessions for up to 30 days, until that record expires.
  • Consent: AI processing happens only after you give explicit consent in the app. You can withdraw consent at any time in the app settings; AI processing of your data stops immediately, and the rest of the app keeps working.
  • No model training: your Garmin data, and any prompts, outputs or insights derived from it, are never used to train, evaluate, benchmark or improve any AI model, and are not kept for that purpose.

4. Who processes your data

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with third parties for their own use. The following service providers process data only on our behalf, to run the app:

Provider Role
Garmin International, Inc. Source of your Garmin data, and recipient of workouts you send to your device
ngrok, Inc. Encrypted connection that carries data between Garmin, the app and our server. Our server and database run on computers we operate; no other hosting provider stores your data.
Anthropic, PBC AI coaching: analysis, conversations, training recommendations and the coach's memory (only with your consent)

If we add or change a provider, we will update this list before it receives your data.

5. Data retention

We keep your Garmin data while your Garmin account is connected to Talaria. If you disconnect Garmin (in the app or in Garmin Connect) or delete your account, we delete your Garmin tokens and the Garmin data we stored about you.

Data Kept
Conversations with the coach until you delete the conversation or your account
What the coach remembers about you until you delete the note or your account
Internal record of each conversation session (what the coach was given and answered, used to continue the session) 30 days after the session ends
Technical record of each coach reply (cost, timing, safety checks; no message text) 180 days
Safety alerts the coach raised (which alert, never your words) until you delete your account
Your training plan and its versions until you delete your account

When you delete your account, we delete all of the above.

6. Your rights and choices

  • Access and export: request a copy of your data.
  • Correction: ask us to correct inaccurate data.
  • Deletion: delete your data at any time by disconnecting Garmin in the app, or by contacting us.
  • Withdraw consent: disconnect Talaria in the app or in Garmin Connect (Settings → Account Information / connected apps), change which data you share there, or turn off AI processing in the app.

To make a request, contact us at talaria.privacy@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

7. Security

Data is transferred over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Garmin credentials and app secrets are kept on our servers only, never on your device, and your Garmin tokens are encrypted in our database. Access to stored data is limited to what is needed to run the app.

8. Children

Talaria is not intended for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data. The AI coach is available only to adults (18 and over).

9. Changes

We will post changes to this policy on this page and update the date above. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
